# SEO/AEO Traffic Strategy — QR Code Agent

**Domain:** qrcodeagent.net  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Context:** Strategic recommendations for growing organic search and answer-engine traffic.

---

## What You Already Have (Strong)

**qrcodeagent.net** is well set up for organic and AI discovery:

- **47 unique static pages** with proper titles, meta, canonical, hreflang, FAQ + HowTo schema
- **Hub-and-spoke architecture** (type / industry / use-case / learn)
- **10 learn articles**, including high-value comparison content like [static vs dynamic QR codes](https://qrcodeagent.net/learn/static-vs-dynamic-qr-codes)
- **AEO hygiene**: `llms.txt`, explicit AI crawler allows in `robots.txt`, server-rendered copy (AI crawlers don't run JS)
- **Clear differentiators** baked into copy: free, no sign-up, logo, SVG export, client-side privacy

Your own audit nailed the diagnosis: on-page and GEO structure are solid. The bottleneck is **authority and distribution**, not more thin pages.

---

## The Reality Check on Head Terms

Your homepage targets ~1M MSV with **KD 96**. You won't win "qr code generator" on-page alone against QR Monkey, Canva, and Adobe.

**Win the long tail first** — these are in your `pages.json` with much better odds:

| Page | MSV | KD | Why it's winnable |
|------|-----|----|-------------------|
| `/qr-codes-for-business-cards` | 5,400 | 37 | High intent, product fits perfectly |
| `/wifi-qr-code` | 1,300 | 33 | Type-specific tool + guide content |
| `/vcard-qr-code` | 1,300 | 33 | Same |
| `/google-review-qr-code` | 1,900 | 40 | Local business intent |
| `/menu-qr-code` | — | — | Restaurant cluster (huge use case) |

Strategy: rank on **modifiers** (`free`, `with logo`, `no sign up`, `svg`) and **type/use-case pages**, then let authority flow to `/`.

## Highest-Impact Moves (Ranked)

### 1. International expansion (biggest untapped lever)

You have 11 locales configured but only **3 in the live sitemap** (EN, DE, ES). Your brief validated huge non-English demand:

- **DE**: 165K MSV for "qr code generator"
- **ID**: 135K MSV (English head term dominates)
- **PT-BR**: 49.5K MSV
- **JA**: 27K MSV

Ship locales in this order: **DE → ID → PT-BR → JA → PL**. That's 47 pages × 8 more locales ≈ **376 new indexable URLs** on a domain you already own — without writing new English content.

### 2. Finish the taxonomy gaps (~8–10 pages)

Still missing from your SEO brief:

- `svg-qr-code-generator` / `colored-qr-code-generator` (real differentiators)
- `google-maps-qr-code`, `paypal-qr-code` (competitors rank on type pages like this)
- Industry pages: `bars`, `coffee-shops`, `gyms`, `salons-spas`, `nonprofits`, `food-trucks`
- Use cases: `promotions`, `flyers-posters`, `table-tents`

Each page should have genuinely unique copy in the benefits/FAQ blocks — not template swaps.

### 3. Comparison & decision content (AEO gold)

Comparison articles take ~**33% of AI citations**. You have one great piece (static vs dynamic). Add:

- "Best free QR code generators (2026)" — honest, positions you without being spammy
- "QR Code Agent vs QR Monkey" — feature table, no fake ratings
- "When to use dynamic QR codes (and when not to)" — links to your static positioning
- "QR code for Google Reviews: step-by-step"

These feed ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews with citable Q&A blocks you already schema-mark well.

### 4. Off-site authority (the actual bottleneck)

Your audit said it clearly: **brand mentions correlate ~3× more with AI citation than backlinks**. For a new domain, traffic won't scale until people talk about the tool elsewhere.

Concrete plays:

| Channel | Action |
|---------|--------|
| **Open source** | Your [GitHub repo](https://github.com/mykpono/qr-code-agent) is a link asset — README should link to qrcodeagent.net, mention "free, no sign-up" |
| **Product directories** | AlternativeTo, SaaSHub, ToolFinder, "free QR generator" roundups |
| **Communities** | r/smallbusiness, r/restaurateur, Indie Hackers, Hacker News "Show HN" |
| **Content partnerships** | Guest posts on restaurant/hospitality blogs linking to your menu + WiFi guides |
| **LinkedIn** | You're already credited in `llms.txt` — post the privacy angle ("nothing uploaded") |

### 5. Product improvements that convert traffic → usage → links

SEO gets visitors; the product keeps them and earns word-of-mouth:

- **Multi-type widget** (your design brief) — WiFi/vCard/WhatsApp forms make type pages genuinely better than content-only competitors
- **Templates rail** — shareable branded presets ("Instagram theme", "Restaurant menu")
- **"Save design"** — localStorage saves without gating (protects your "no sign-up" keyword)

A tool people actually use gets bookmarked, shared, and linked. A content-only landing page doesn't.

### 6. Technical quick wins

- **Self-host fonts** (still on Google Fonts) — CWV / LCP improvement
- **Google Search Console** — submit sitemap, monitor which pages get impressions vs. clicks
- **Core Web Vitals** on mobile (most QR traffic is phone)
- **Entity signals**: `Organization` schema with `sameAs` (LinkedIn, GitHub, lab.mykpono.com)

## AEO-Specific Playbook

You're already ahead on structure. Double down on:

1. **Answer-first paragraphs** — your learn articles do this well ("The short answer" upfront)
2. **Tables** — AI engines love extractable comparisons (you have one on static vs dynamic)
3. **Honest limitations** — "we make static codes only" builds trust and gets cited over competitors who oversell
4. **Keep `llms.txt` in sync** — you have a build guard for this; good
5. **Brand consistency** — always "QR Code Agent" + qrcodeagent.net so LLMs can attribute citations
